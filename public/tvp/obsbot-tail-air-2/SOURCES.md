All spoken and on-screen facts come from first-hand official pages checked for the 2026-11-22 production brief. This film only reviews official launch materials and official demo footage. It is not a hands-on test.

## Facts used
- Official launch date: 2026-11-19.
- 1-inch CMOS, 4K up to 60 fps, 1080p up to 120 fps.
- OBSBOT Log described as the company’s first Log curve on its cameras; custom 3D LUT supported.
- AI Tracking 2.0: people, 30+ animals, 200+ objects.
- Lens module rotates 90 degrees; official wording: native 4K vertical up to 60 fps without cropping the full imaging area; auto leveling.
- Preorder started 2026-11-04 with a stated $100 discount; planned on-sale date 2026-12-08.
- U.S. official store, this round: NDI Activated preorder $899, struck-through $999. Non-NDI list price was not verified; not spoken.

## Facts withheld
- 13-stop dynamic range (HDR condition) and 205-minute battery (1080p/30 condition) — not spoken unconditionally.
- Tail 2 ADV zoom or other ADV-only specs.
- RMB or other regional prices that were not first-hand verified.
- Any claim of unboxing, tracking feel, or image quality vs other cameras.
